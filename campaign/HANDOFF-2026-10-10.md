# Handoff: finite-β free-boundary single-stage stellarator optimization in VMEX (2026-10-10)

## 1. What this project is

We optimize the **coils and the plasma together** (single stage) with **VMEX**, a JAX/GPU VMEC
reimplementation, at **finite β (⟨β⟩ = 2.5%) with a self-consistent bootstrap current**. There are two arms.
We always report the two arms side by side, with every metric taken from the free-boundary solve of the final coils.

- **Free arm (our method):** a free-boundary equilibrium with the **three-term boundary condition**
  - B·n = 0, pressure balance, and sheet current K = 0 on the last closed flux surface (LCFS);
  - solved by virtual casing (`ThreeTermFreeBoundaryProblem` in `vmex/core/freeboundary_vc.py`;
    `FreeBoundaryProblem(boundary_condition="three_term")`);
  - the plasma boundary is not prescribed: the coils produce it.
- **Fixed arm (comparison):** a fixed-boundary equilibrium plus a coil B·n term, the classic single stage.
- **Optimizer:** plain SciPy SLSQP with hard constraints, on exact implicit-differentiation gradients:
  - block-tridiagonal factorization, Newton certification of every solve, and a grouped pullback;
  - no penalty weights to tune except the objective;
  - every step is saved: `wout.stepN.nc`, `coils.stepN.json` and a `metrics.jsonl` row.
- **Bootstrap current:**
  - Redl (QA, QH): solved with the equilibrium, sampled across the surfaces.
  - DKX Fokker–Planck kinetic (QI): its 50-surface loop is a `jax.lax.map`, which cut compile time 1016 s → 40 s.
- **Resolution:** 8×8 modes, ns = 51 for optimization; ns = 201 for the dense post-processing re-solve.

### Constraints available, all hard SLSQP rows

- **Core rows:**
  - total ι floor (and optional ceiling); vacuum-ι floor;
  - major radius R0 = 1 m ± 1 mm; axis |B| = 1 T ± 1e-3;
  - aspect-ratio range;
  - bootstrap self-consistency (Redl mismatch);
  - QI mirror ratio.
- **Mercier:** D_Merc_min ≥ 0.02 on s ≥ 0.2 (near-axis D_Merc is unreliable; Landreman et al. 2026, arXiv:2606.19523).
- **Bootstrap-current minimization:** a loss term w/2·ι_I², where ι_I = |μ0 I| R0 / (2π a² B_axis).
- **Coil engineering limits:**
  - length, curvature and mean-squared curvature, scaled from (1.8, 2.5, 1.2) × the plasma-plus-clearance radius;
  - coil–coil distance (QA 0.10 m);
  - **coil–LCFS distance (QA ≥ 0.20 m, QH/QI 0.15 m)**.

### Driver CLI options

- `--vacuum-iota-floor`, `--iota-ceiling`, `--aspect-range LOW HIGH` (the coil limits stay at the case's);
- `--current-iota-weight`, `--design-step-scale`;
- `--seed-run <dir>`, `--restart <dir with input.run, coils.json, wout.nc>`;
- `COIL_CASE` environment variable: `qa4-beta`, `qi6-beta`, `qh4-beta`, with suffixes `-merc`, `-jbs`, `-tok`, `-A<aspect>`.

### Speed: exact, with no accuracy change

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Free QA steady step | 265 s | 105 s |
| Fixed-arm grouped plasma Jacobian | 116 s | 15.5 s (exact to 1.2e-14) |

  - `extra/fedge.py`: fixed-boundary re-solve. It gives fedge = 0 by construction, so it is meaningless for the free arm;

| | Floor 0.27, step 267 | Floor 0.29, step 242 |
|---|---|---|
| QA error | 5.5e-4 | 7.0e-4 |
| ι axis → edge (max) | 0.249 → 0.461 (0.485) | 0.262 → 0.471 (0.493) |
| Aspect ratio | 4.11 | 4.04 |
| Toroidal current | 86 kA | 85 kA |
| B·n / pressure balance / K | 2.8e-4 / 1.6e-4 / 3.3e-4 | 2.3e-4 / 1.7e-4 / 3.0e-4 |
| Alpha loss | 0.60% ± 0.35% | **0%** |
| ε_eff (s = 0.1–1) | 0.024–0.134% | 0.025–0.144% |
| Max-J fraction (all pitches) | 63% | 62% |

- **Both cases:** ⟨β⟩ = 2.57%, Redl mismatch about 1e-5, D_Merc > 0 on s ≥ 0.2, magnetic well throughout.
  - D_R turns slightly unstable at s ≈ 0.85–0.95.
  - Max-J: deeply trapped particles are anti-max-J (B_min falls outward), and the edge region s > 0.9 is too.
- **Mercier floor:** it costs QA. With vacuum floor 0.23, the plateau is 8e-4 against 2.9e-4 without it, and alpha loss is
  3.2% against 0%. Raising the vacuum floor to 0.27–0.29 restores 0–0.6% loss.
- **Bootstrap weight scan (w = 0.01 / 0.1 / 1):**
  - It lowers ι_I mainly by raising the vacuum ι.
  - At w = 1: ι_I 0.157, but QA error is 10× worse, the run is pinned at A 3.5, and total ι is about 0.6, past the 1/2
    resonance (that run has no ι ceiling).
- **QI:** unscaled steps (`--design-step-scale 1`) reach much lower QI (0.03 against 0.12). At larger aspect, QI A8
  reaches 5.9e-3 and QH A8 7.5e-3.

## 6. Planned comparison with the augmented-Lagrangian paper (not started; waiting on the user)

- **Papers:**
  - Gil, Li, Stratton, Kaptanoglu & Stenson, **PRL 137, 065101 (2026)**, https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/n7gk-922h
  - Companion **PRE 114, 025202 (2026)**, arXiv:2507.12681.
- **Their method:**
  - It is **stage two only**: coils for a *fixed, prescribed vacuum boundary*. It uses an augmented-Lagrangian outer loop
    with L-BFGS-B inside, in SIMSOPT, with the flux constraint ⟨B·n⟩/⟨B⟩ and engineering thresholds.
  - It has no finite β, no bootstrap current, no free-boundary or plasma-response check, and its QS is evaluated on the
    fixed target.
  - About 40 min per run on one laptop CPU core.
- **Their cases:**
  - Landreman–Paul QA (nfp 2, A 6): 3 and 4 coils per half period, against Wechsung L18/L20/L24.
    Best ⟨B·n⟩/⟨B⟩ ≈ 2e-5 to 9e-4; QS 2e-3 to 1e-5.
  - LP QH (nfp 4, A 8): 4 and 5 coils.
  - Stellaris QI (nfp 4, A 9.8): 5 and 6 coils; alpha loss 0%.
  - W7-X, HSX and a dipole QA.
- **Their metric set** (match it): ⟨B·n⟩/⟨B⟩, max B·n/B, max force (MN/m), coil length per half period, minimum
  coil–coil and coil–surface distance, max κ, max MSC, QS error, HTS length. Alpha losses use SIMPLE: 1000 particles,
  3.5 MeV, r = 0.2, 0.2 s.
- **Their thresholds** (Kaptanoglu, at R = 1 m): κ ≤ 12 m⁻¹, coil length ≤ 6.5 m, coil–surface ≥ 16.6 cm, coil–coil ≥ 8.3 cm.

  - The equilibrium is the actual free-boundary one at finite β with a self-consistent bootstrap current.

  - Constraints are hard: no weight tuning.
